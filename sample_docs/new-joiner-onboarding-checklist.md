# New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

## First day
1. Collect your laptop and ID badge from the Facilities desk on the ground floor.
2. Sign in to your laptop with the temporary password sent to your personal email and change it.
3. Set up multi-factor authentication using the Password Reset and MFA Guide.
4. Complete the mandatory Information Security and POSH training in the Learning Portal.

## First week
1. Submit bank details, PAN and address proof in the HR Portal under "My Profile".
2. Enrol in health insurance within 15 days of joining. Dependants can be added only during this window.
3. Meet your buddy and reporting manager to agree your 30-60-90 day goals.
4. Request access to team tools such as the Analytics Workspace through the IT Service Portal.

## Contacts
HR Operations (hr.ops@example.com) handles payroll and documentation questions. Facilities handles seating and ID badges. The IT Service Desk handles laptops, access and accounts.
