# Compensation Review Guidelines (HR Confidential)

## Review cycle Project Banyan
The annual compensation review, code-named Project Banyan, runs from 1 April to 30 April. HR business partners prepare increment recommendations using the merit matrix approved by the leadership team.

## Merit matrix
Employees rated "Exceeds" receive an increment band of 10 to 14 percent. Employees rated "Meets" receive 6 to 9 percent. Employees rated "Below" are not eligible for a merit increment and are placed on a development plan.

## Confidentiality
Increment recommendations must not be shared with employees or managers before the official letters are released. Files are stored only in the HR confidential library.
