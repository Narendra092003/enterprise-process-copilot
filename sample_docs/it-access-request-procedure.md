# Analytics Workspace Access Procedure

## Purpose
This procedure explains how employees request access to the Analytics Workspace (Power BI premium workspace and the reporting data warehouse). Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed every quarter.

## Request access
1. Open the IT Service Portal and choose "Access Request > Analytics Workspace".
2. Select the workspace name and the role you need: Viewer, Contributor or Admin.
3. Enter a business justification of at least one sentence and the project code.
4. Choose a duration: 30, 60 or 90 days. Permanent access is not available.
5. Submit the request. It is routed to your reporting manager for approval.
6. After manager approval, the workspace owner approves Contributor or Admin roles.
7. You receive an email when access is provisioned, usually within 1 business day.

## Roles
Viewer can open reports and dashboards. Contributor can publish reports and edit datasets. Admin can manage workspace members and is limited to two people per workspace.

## Renewal and removal
Access expires automatically at the end of the selected duration. Raise a new request seven days before expiry to avoid interruption. Access is removed on the last working day when an employee leaves or changes teams.
