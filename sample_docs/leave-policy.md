# Leave Policy

## Annual leave entitlement
Full-time employees receive 18 days of earned leave, 8 days of casual leave and 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Earned leave accrues monthly at 1.5 days per month. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

## Applying for leave
1. Apply in the HR Portal under "Leave > Apply" at least 5 working days before planned leave of 3 days or more.
2. Casual leave of 1 to 2 days can be applied on the same day before 10:00 AM.
3. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate uploaded within 3 days of returning.
4. Your reporting manager approves or rejects the request in the HR Portal.

## Carry forward and encashment
Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year. Casual and sick leave cannot be carried forward. Earned leave above the carry-forward limit can be encashed in March payroll.

## Maternity, paternity and bereavement leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per statutory requirements. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption. Bereavement leave is 5 working days for an immediate family member.
