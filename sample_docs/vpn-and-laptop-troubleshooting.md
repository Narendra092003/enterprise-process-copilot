# VPN and Laptop Troubleshooting Guide

## VPN not connecting
1. Check that your internet connection works by opening any public website.
2. Restart the GlobalConnect VPN client and sign in again with your company email.
3. Approve the multi-factor authentication prompt on your phone within 60 seconds.
4. If you see error 809 or "gateway unreachable", switch to the backup gateway "vpn2.example.com" in the client settings.
5. If it still fails, restart the laptop and try once more before raising a ticket.

## Laptop running slow
1. Restart the laptop at least once a week so updates can install.
2. Close browser tabs and applications you are not using.
3. Run "Software Center > Health Check" to clear temporary files.
4. If disk space is below 10 GB free, move personal files to OneDrive.

## Outlook or Teams not syncing
Sign out of Outlook and Teams, clear the Teams cache from Software Center, and sign back in. If emails are still not arriving after 30 minutes, raise a ticket with the Service Desk.

## Raising a ticket
Raise an IT ticket in the IT Service Portal under "Incident" with a screenshot of the error. Priority 1 issues such as a complete outage can be reported by phone on extension 4000.
