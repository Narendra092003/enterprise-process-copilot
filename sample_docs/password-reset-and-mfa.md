# Password Reset and MFA Guide

## Reset your password
1. Go to the self-service password reset page at passwordreset.example.com.
2. Enter your company email and complete the verification code sent to your registered phone.
3. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters with no part of your name.
4. Wait 5 minutes, then sign in again on your laptop while connected to the office network or VPN.

## Account locked out
Your account locks for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or use the self-service reset page to unlock immediately.

## Password rules
Passwords expire every 90 days. You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords. The Service Desk will never ask you for your password over phone, chat or email.

## Setting up MFA on a new phone
1. Sign in to mysignins.example.com from your laptop.
2. Select "Security info > Add method > Authenticator app".
3. Scan the QR code with the Authenticator app on your new phone.
4. Remove the old phone from the list once the new one works.
