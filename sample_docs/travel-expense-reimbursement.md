# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible expenses
Business travel, client meals, local conveyance for client visits, and approved training fees are reimbursable. Personal expenses, alcohol, fines and upgrades without approval are not reimbursable.

## Daily limits
Domestic hotel stay is capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night in other cities. Meal allowance is INR 1,200 per day on travel days. Airport taxi or cab fares are reimbursed at actuals with a receipt.

## How to submit an expense claim
1. Log in to the Expense Portal and select "New Claim".
2. Add each expense line with the date, category, amount and project code.
3. Upload a clear photo or PDF of every receipt above INR 500.
4. Submit the claim within 30 days of the expense date. Late claims need finance controller approval.
5. Your reporting manager approves the claim, then Finance verifies it.
6. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle.

## Who approves expenses
Claims up to INR 25,000 are approved by the reporting manager. Claims above INR 25,000 also need approval from the department head. Finance Shared Services (finance.helpdesk@example.com) answers questions about rejected claims.
