# Vendor Onboarding Process

## Steps to onboard a new vendor
1. The requesting team raises a "New Vendor" request in the Procurement Portal with the vendor name, services and estimated annual spend.
2. Procurement sends the vendor the registration form, NDA and bank details form.
3. The vendor uploads GST certificate, PAN, cancelled cheque and signed NDA.
4. Finance verifies bank details with a test transaction of INR 1.
5. Information Security completes a risk assessment for vendors who will access company data.
6. Procurement activates the vendor code, usually within 7 business days.

Purchases above INR 5,00,000 need three competitive quotes. Purchases above INR 25,00,000 need approval from the Chief Financial Officer.
